Formulas

Calculations used in this dashboard.

📐 Formula Sheet

Energy (kWh) = Power (kW) × Time (hours)

Hourly, daily and weekly energy totals.

Normalized Use (kWh/m²) = Building kWh ÷ Floor Area (m²)

Fair benchmarking between buildings of different size.

Potential Waste Energy (kWh) = Σ (Load of empty rooms with lights/HVAC ON × Hours ON)

Applied only to rooms flagged by the waste rule.

CO₂ Waste (kg) = Wasted Energy (kWh) × Emission Factor (kg CO₂/kWh)

Emission factor depends on the local grid.

Cost Savings (₹) = Wasted Energy (kWh) × Tariff (₹/kWh)

Tariff as per your electricity bill.

Building Share (%) = Building kWh ÷ Total kWh × 100

Used for the Buildings pie chart.
